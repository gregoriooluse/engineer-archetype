# How this workshop is managed.
---
an offline inbox will contain all ideas and articles that I have in mind.
Also Projects, creative work, unpublished ideas and narrative works will remain offline, until I see fit that they become worthy of public access...

# steps
Notes are quick thoughts passing thoughts and ideas that spark a line of thought, some seem worthy of further exploring.

Exploring is where the notes are workshopped researched and benchmarked to see if a further inquiry remains, is the question still burning, interesting of captive?

Writing is where the well explored ideas take form into a formal article or long form post and are developed into a finished product something worthy of storage/filing/ preservation (there is a word for this) 
in essay form the close to final version ends up here and is kept as precious resuorce to behold :) , reference, hyperlink and maybe even turn into, incorporate into or develop into a greater project or product.

creative work, simply where all of my creative ideas form and become a world to explore.

projects are where I share the few worthwhile projects and side bars that presently occupy my time and mind and share meaningful progress plans and future visions.
